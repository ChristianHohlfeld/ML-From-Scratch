What is actually modeled?

3D acoustic pressure in an elliptical, shape-adjustable tube with reflecting walls and end damping. Broadband excitation at the inlet; acoustic microphone at the outlet. Each triangle-mesh vertex has an independently deformable XYZ position, an inertial mass and axial/angular spring coupling. Local voxel pressure pushes along the surface normal; the resulting surface flux changes local pressure at each acoustic timestep. Linearized moving-boundary FSI still uses fixed air voxels, so this is NOT full finite-deformation fluid remeshing, contact or real vocal-tract validation.
