Research hypothesis: The vocal apparatus imposes structure on outgoing sound. Are there stable geometric and topological invariants of that sound across different speakers and pitch? Here we test only a first approximation, and compare it against a strong, simple spectral control.
Vocal-fold vibration is the source of voiced sounds; the vocal tract filters those waves. Unvoiced consonants are different. The visual 3D coordinates are time-delay embeddings, not pressure sampled at 3D locations. Reference: your 3D wave-machine work.
