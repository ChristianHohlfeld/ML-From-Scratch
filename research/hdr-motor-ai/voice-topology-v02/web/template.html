Our machine · adjustable soft form

Learning modifies only tube geometry. Wave propagation is always calculated by the 3D solver. Topology is not inferred automatically.

What is actually modeled?

3D acoustic pressure in an elliptical, shape-adjustable tube with reflecting walls and end damping. Broadband excitation at the inlet; acoustic microphone at the outlet. Boundary motion occurs between simulations (quasi-static), not as a fully coupled moving soft body.
